    How to play

    • Tap one of your pieces, then a glowing square to move it. Red rings mark captures.
    • Drag to orbit the camera. Pinch or scroll to zoom.
    • Captures play out as duels. Tap during one to speed it up.
    • The candle shows a suggested move. The curved arrow takes back your last move and the reply.
    • Games are saved on this device after every move.
